Guiding Principles

Our Vision, Mission & Core Values

The enduring bedrock upon which all teaching, mentoring, and student life at Sanskar is nurtured.

Our Vision

To be an institution of benchmark excellence that nurtures intellectually curious, ethically grounded, and emotionally resilient global citizens equipped to contribute purposefully to society.

Our Mission

To cultivate critical thinking, scientific temper, and creative expression through experiential pedagogy, supportive mentorship, and state-of-the-art technological resources.

Core Values

Satya (Truth), Shresthata (Excellence), Vinamrata (Humility), and Sahayata (Compassion) guide our student body, faculty, and administrative ethics.

Milestones

Our Journey of Excellence

Key milestones that mark the growth and development of Sanskar Public School.

2001

Foundation of Sanskar

Inaugurated with 120 students and 8 dedicated educators with a focus on value-driven primary education.

2009

CBSE Affiliation & Senior Secondary Wing

Accredited for Senior Secondary certification in Science, Commerce, and Humanities with brand new laboratory wings.

2018

STEM Robotics Lab & Sports Complex

Addition of international-standard athletic tracks, computer robotics center, and digital smart interactive boards.

Present

Over 1000+ Enrolled Learners

Ranked among the premier holistic educational institutions with 100% board examination results and national olympiad accolades.
