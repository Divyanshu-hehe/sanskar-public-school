Modern Infrastructure

Designed to Empower Every Learner

Explore the purpose-built learning spaces and student amenities across our sprawling green campus.

Spacious Smart Classrooms

Air-cooled, ergonomically designed classrooms featuring natural sunlight, interactive touch boards, high-speed audio-visual aids, and comfortable seating that accommodates up to 35 students per section for optimal teacher-student engagement.

Advanced Science Laboratories

Dedicated, fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories compliant with CBSE safety standards. Students engage in practical demonstrations with precision microscopes, glass apparatus, optical benches, and chemical workbenches.

Central Knowledge Library

A haven of quiet study boasting over 15,000 fiction and non-fiction titles, reference encyclopedias, national research periodicals, and an automated e-learning section with kindle e-readers and high-speed internet access.

Computer & AI Innovation Lab

Equipped with high-performance desktop terminals, gigabit fiber broadband, robotics microcontrollers (Arduino/Raspberry Pi), and industry-standard coding platforms where students learn Python, Scratch, and digital design.

Sports Arena & Athletic Complex

A multi-sport complex comprising a 200m running track, regulation football ground, cricket nets, basketball and volleyball courts, table tennis hall, and yoga pavilion overseen by NIS-qualified physical instructors.

Campus Safety, GPS Buses & Health

Gated security with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, fire-safety certified infrastructure, pure RO drinking water coolers on every floor, modern hygiene-sanitized restrooms, GPS bus fleet, and on-campus medical infirmary with resident nurse.
