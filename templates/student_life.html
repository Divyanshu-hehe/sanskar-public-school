Clubs & Co-Curricular Societies

Over 15 student-led interest clubs encourage passion projects, innovation, and self-discovery.

Robotics & Innovation Club

Hands-on micro-soldering, sensor automation, drone piloting, and preparation for national science olympiads.

Model United Nations (MUN), parliamentary debate rounds, creative writing workshops, and poetry slams.

Oil canvas painting, clay sculpting, terracotta pottery wheels, and annual campus art galleries.

Blood donation awareness, peer literacy tutoring for underprivileged learners, and book donation drives.
