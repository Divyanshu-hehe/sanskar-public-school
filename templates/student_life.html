Sports & Wellness

Physical Fitness & Athletic Development

Instilling sportsmanship, physical endurance, tactical teamwork, and personal health habits.

Athletics & Track Sports

Sprint relays, long jump, high jump, and obstacle training under the guidance of qualified physical instructors.

Football & Outdoor Games

Spacious playground facilities for football, cricket nets, volleyball, and cooperative sports activities.

Yoga & Mindful Breathing

Daily morning exercises, Suryanamaskar, posture alignment, and mindfulness sessions to enhance focus and mental calm.

Arts & Culture

Creative Expression & Cultural Activities

Encouraging artistic discovery, public speaking confidence, and Indian cultural heritage.

Visual Arts & Craft

Watercolors, sketching, paper craft, poster making, and creative exhibitions that allow children's imaginations to flourish.

Music & Performing Arts

Vocal classical prayers, patriotic melodies, harmonium, tabla, and traditional cultural dance performances.

Elocution & Recitation

Poetry recitation, speech competitions, Hindi and English debates, and storytelling sessions for confidence on stage.

Morning Assembly & Shlokas

Daily morning gathering with national anthem, Sanskrit shlokas, moral stories, thought for the day, and news presentation.

Eco-Awareness & Gardening

Tree plantation drives, campus cleanliness routines, water conservation awareness, and botanical appreciation.

National Days & Celebrations

Joyous celebrations of Independence Day, Republic Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Saraswati Puja, and the Annual Day extravaganza.
