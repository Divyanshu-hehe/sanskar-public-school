Policies & Institutional Guidelines

Ensuring transparency, student safety, academic integrity, and inclusive standards.

Home / Policies

1. Privacy Policy

Sanskar Public School is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of students, parents, and visitors. Information collected through online admission inquiries, registration forms, and website analytics is utilized solely for institutional evaluation, academic administration, and official communication.

We never sell, rent, or disclose personal contact details or sensitive student records to unauthorized third parties or commercial marketers.

2. Website Terms & Conditions

All content, including school branding, logos, circulars, photographs, and academic syllabi displayed on this website, are the intellectual property of Sanskar Public School. Unauthorized replication, framing, or commercial reproduction without written authorization is strictly prohibited.

Online admission submissions do not automatically guarantee enrollment; admission is subject to seat vacancy, document verification, and fee clearance.

3. Student Code of Conduct & Discipline

Aligned with our motto "विद्या ददाति विनयम्", students are expected to uphold decorum, mutual respect, and academic honesty. Bullying, ragging, vandalism of campus property, or cyber misconduct is treated under a strict zero-tolerance policy.

  • Punctuality in attending morning assemblies and scheduled classes is mandatory.
  • Prescribed school uniform, proper grooming, and student identity cards must be worn daily.
  • Use of unauthorized mobile phones during class hours is strictly restricted.

4. Attendance Policy

In adherence to CBSE guidelines, students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance during the academic session to be eligible for annual examinations. Absence exceeding three consecutive days requires a formal medical certificate or written leave note signed by parents.

5. Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy

The emotional, psychological, and physical well-being of every student is our paramount priority. All staff members, support crews, and bus drivers undergo rigorous police background verification.

Our internal Child Protection Committee (CPC) and POSH grievance cell ensure a secure, respectful, and transparent atmosphere across all classrooms and transport buses.

6. Website Accessibility Statement

We strive to conform to World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. Features include high-contrast color palettes, descriptive alternative image tags, keyboard-navigable tabs, and reduced-motion support.
